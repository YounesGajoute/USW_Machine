# v2.0.0-dev.6 — Calibrated centring and initialization correction

This Version 2 development checkpoint includes the centring firmware, backend,
tests, calibration captures and deployment archives accumulated since dev.5.
The installed Nano firmware is **v2.1.7-cal-departure**.

## Behavior

- Both jaws open toward HOME with increasing PWM and close toward TRAVEL with
  decreasing PWM. Inputs are active LOW. Upper: D2/J1, HOME D3, TRAVEL D4.
  Lower: D9/J2, HOME A1, TRAVEL A0.
- Measured HOME/TRAVEL endpoints: upper **2118/1262 µs**, lower **1545/789 µs**.
- The saved gap model contributes **0.2 mm per jaw at TRAVEL**, with zero
  mechanical offset, for a **0.4 mm minimum total gap**.
- Successful gap moves retain servo PWM for position holding while connected.
  This is position control, not a commanded maximum torque or measured force.
- Departure from an asserted endpoint uses the calibration rate of at most
  4 µs per 40 ms with initial settling. The 240 µs release allowance persists
  across retries and interruptions until the origin switch releases stably.
- Raw destination contact inhibits movement immediately. Conflicting HOME and
  TRAVEL on one jaw, E-stop, failed motion and disconnect release PWM.
- Initialization prevents overlapping setup invocations, validates reference
  gaps against the Nano's calibrated reach, verifies HOME before applying the
  reference gap, and guards against stale reference-selection callbacks.
- Earlier saved iterations include flash optimization, bounded network handling,
  switch protections, diagnostic calibration firmware and regression coverage.

## Limits and commissioning

Gap and intermediate angles are calculated from commanded PWM, not continuously
measured. STATUS does not expose an explicit PWM-attached/holding flag, and a
previous targetH can remain after HOME. Success status alone is not an electrical
measurement of holding torque or a measurement of the physical gap.

The broader machineSetup test fixture had five failures both before and after
the earlier backend correction; this checkpoint does not claim the entire
repository test suite passes. Mechanical force, thermal duty and complete
production-cycle commissioning remain unverified. This is a development
pre-release; the Version 1 stable line is unchanged.
