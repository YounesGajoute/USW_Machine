RECONNECT AND ERROR HANDLING
- SESSION_REPLACED or changed Master boot: discard all old authority, perform discovery/registration again, reset session-scoped comparisons.
- STALE_BINDING: discard stale observation and fetch current state.
- REPORT_CONFLICT/REPORT_OUT_OF_ORDER: diagnose ordering; do not reuse an accepted sequence for changed content.
- STALE_SLAVE_BOOT: old boot must not restore a retired session.
- LEDGER_CHANGED or sticky discontinuity hold: surface operator review; do not clear automatically.
- 401/403: source-address, identity or optional-authentication fault, no fallback.
- 426: incompatible contract, no fallback.
- 503 AUTH_NOT_CONFIGURED/SYNC_NOT_CONFIGURED: Master commissioning is not configured, not a statement about reference requirement.
- 503 SYNC_UNAVAILABLE/network timeout: unknown authority, bounded retries.
Use 1s state polling, 2s acknowledgement heartbeat and 3s request timeout; retry 250ms exponentially to 10s with jitter. There are no live grants to renew in this stage.
